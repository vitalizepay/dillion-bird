Cookie policy

The cookies this site uses, what each one does, and how to turn the optional ones off.

Cookies are small files a website stores on your device. Some are needed for the site to work. Others tell us how the site is being used. This page explains which ones we use and how to turn them off.

What we use

TypeWhat it doesCan you refuse it?
EssentialRemembers your cookie choice and keeps the site working as you move between pages.No. Without it the site cannot remember that you declined the rest.
AnalyticsCounts visits and shows which pages and services people actually read, so we know what to improve. Reports are aggregated; we do not use them to identify you.Yes.
MarketingWould let us measure whether an advert or campaign brought someone to the site.Yes. These are off unless you switch them on.

Your choice

When you first arrive, you are asked whether to allow analytics and marketing cookies. Nothing beyond the essential cookie is set until you choose. You can change your answer at any time.

Change cookie preferences

Controlling cookies in your browser

Every browser lets you block or delete cookies, usually under Settings, then Privacy. Blocking all cookies will affect how most websites behave, not only this one.

Third parties

Some pages embed content from other services, such as a map or a video player. Those services may set their own cookies once the content loads, under their own policies.

Changes

If we add or remove a cookie, this page is updated and the date at the top changes.
